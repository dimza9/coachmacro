Coach Macro — Privacy Policy and Contact

Last update: 13 October 2026 · Developer: Dmitry Zayts

Summary. Coach Macro does not collect your personal data. The developer does not operate a server and does not receive your data. The app has no account, no advertisements, no analytics, and no tracking.

Data on your iPhone

The app keeps your meals, weight, goals, recipes, ingredients, and settings on your iPhone. When you delete the app, iOS deletes this data.

Apple Health

If you give permission, the app reads these values from Apple Health: active energy, resting energy, workouts, steps, and body weight. The app shows these values next to your nutrition goals.

If you give permission, the app writes these values to Apple Health: body weight, dietary energy, protein, fat, and carbohydrates.

The Health data stays on your device and in Apple Health. The app does not send Health data to the developer or to other companies. You can change these permissions at any time in the Health app.

iCloud

  • iCloud backup (optional): If you set it to on, the app keeps a copy of your data in your own iCloud account. Only you have access to this copy.
  • Apple Watch: The app sends your daily budget to your Apple Watch through your private iCloud database. The Watch uses the same database to send the snacks and weights that you log on the Watch.

Apple operates iCloud. Refer to the Apple privacy policy for iCloud.

Partner sharing (optional)

If you pair with a partner, the app shares your daily macro budget, the meals that you send, and shared leftovers with that partner. The app encrypts this data on your iPhone before it sends the data. Only your iPhone and the iPhone of your partner have the key. The encrypted data goes through the public CloudKit database of the app.

To find the correct records, the app also stores some data without encryption: a random pair ID, anonymous iCloud user IDs that the app gets from Apple, dates, and times. When you unpair, the app deletes the records that you made. If your iPhone has no connection at that time, some records can stay. The records of your partner stay until your partner also unpairs.

Barcode lookup

When you scan a barcode, the app sends the barcode number to Open Food Facts to get the product data. The app sends only the barcode number. Open Food Facts has its own privacy policy. Product data comes from Open Food Facts under the Open Database License (ODbL).

Camera and Reminders

The app uses the camera only to scan barcodes. The app does not keep or send images. The app uses Reminders only when you export a grocery list.

Children

The app does not collect data from any person, and this includes children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page will change.
